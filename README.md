# Power BI Dashboard on India Tourism Growth Trends

## Overview
This project showcases an interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing tourism trends in India. It highlights key metrics such as **domestic and foreign tourist visits, tourism revenue, and regional performance**, helping uncover patterns and growth opportunities in the tourism sector.

---

##  Key Dashboard Features

- 📌 **KPI Cards**
  - Total Domestic Tourist Visits  
  - Total Foreign Tourist Arrivals  
  - Total Tourism Revenue  

- 📈 **Year-wise Trend Analysis**
  - Line charts showing growth/decline in tourism over multiple years  

- 🌍 **State-wise Tourism Distribution**
  - Bar/column charts comparing tourist visits across states  

- 🗺️ **Geographical Insights**
  - Map visualization to identify high-tourism regions in India  

- 📊 **Category-wise Analysis**
  - Breakdown of domestic vs foreign tourists  

- 🎛️ **Interactive Filters**
  - Year selection  
  - Region/state filtering  
  - Drill-down capabilities

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation  
- **Dataset (CSV/Excel)** – Tourism statistics  



## 📌 Key Insights Gained

- 📈 **Consistent Growth Trend**  
  Tourism in India shows an overall increasing trend over the years, with occasional fluctuations.

- 🌍 **Domestic Tourism Dominates**  
  Domestic tourist visits are significantly higher compared to foreign arrivals, indicating strong internal travel demand.

- 🏆 **Top Performing States**  
  Certain states consistently attract the highest number of tourists, making them key contributors to tourism revenue.

- 💰 **Revenue Correlation**  
  Higher tourist footfall directly contributes to increased tourism revenue, highlighting the economic importance of the sector.

- 📉 **Impact of External Factors**  
  Some years show dips in tourism, possibly due to global or national events affecting travel.

- 🗺️ **Regional Imbalance**  
  Tourism is concentrated in specific regions, suggesting opportunities for development in less-visited areas.
